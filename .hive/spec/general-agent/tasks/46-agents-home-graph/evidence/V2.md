- `h` (AgentsHiddenParent): the hidden stopped `t4-verifier-1` shows again, and `t4-review-1` moves from `home` to its rail below `t4-verifier-1` (`╰┨` at level 2). A second `h` moves it back to `home` and shows `h shows 1 stopped`.
- `←` `→` (AgentsHome, AgentsCoordinatorChild, CompactAgentsHome): the selection moves between `home` and the projects. At 80×24, `home` is the last page: `projects 2–2 of 2 · home` and `← hive`. On the first page: `projects 1–2 of 2` and `home →`. The first `←` from `home` selects `manager-api`, which is on the screen, so the page does not move (rule of task 33). In the table, the check `'projects 1–2 of 2 · home':NO` on that row is the expected result.
- `↑` `↓` (AgentsNested): `↑` moves from `t5-research-2` to `t5-research-1`, then to `t5-worker-1`. `↓ ↓ ↓` reaches `t6-worker-1`.
- `enter` (AgentsHome, AgentsNested): the details popup opens. `esc` (AgentDetailsGeneral) closes it.
- `r`: on the Agents graph (AgentsCoordinatorChild) it does nothing. In Health › Agents on `plan-review-1` it shows `[list] restart: coordinator and supervisor only`. On `coordinator-g2` (`↑` ×5) it shows `[list] demo mode: no action`.
