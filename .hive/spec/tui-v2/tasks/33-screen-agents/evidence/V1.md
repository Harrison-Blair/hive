# V1 Tests

## New and changed tests

`internal/tui/agents_screen_test.go`:

- `TestAgentsBoardsMatchTheirGrids`: Main, AgentsMany, AgentDetails, and CompactAgents match their grids cell for cell (AC2).
- `TestAgentsFitEachSize`: 5 data sets (Main, AgentsMany, nine projects, long text, empty) at 79×24, 80×24, 80×25, 119×40, 119×41, 120×40, and 120×41. Each line has the pane width, no border breaks, and the selected agent shows. At 79 columns the screen is the too-small notice (AC2, decision 46).
- `TestAgentsGraphShowsProjectAgentsOnly`: no coordinator or supervisor card, a heavy accent border `┃` on the selected card, and an accent idle dot `◐` (AC1).
- `TestAgentsSelectedCardJoints` (repair): the joint of the selected card is the mixed-weight rune in accent: `┯` below a tree manager, `┷` above a tree child, `┡` on the corner of a rail manager, `┨` beside a rail child.
- `TestAgentsArrowsScrollThePods`, `TestAgentsUpDownMoveInThePod`: sideways scroll keeps the selected card on the screen (AC1).
- `TestAgentsHHidesStoppedAgents`, `TestAgentsDetailsPopupKeys`, `TestAgentsDemoActionsShowTheNotice`, `TestAgentsTOpensTheTask`: `h`, `enter`, `esc`, `a`, `p`, `t`, and no `r` (AC1, AC4).
- `TestAgentsCompactFooter` and the footer checks in `TestAgentsDetailsPopupKeys`: the `[graph]` and `[details]` hints at 120 and 80 columns (AC4).
- `TestAgentsNormalModeLoadsAndRefreshes`: normal mode in a scratch home reads the project, goal, agent, claim, and log records. `R` reloads and keeps the selection (AC3).

`requireWholeAgentsBorders` (repair) also accepts the heavy rail joints and checks that each `┏` has a `┓` and each `┗` has a `┛` in the same line.

`internal/tui/component/card_test.go` (repair): `TestCardSelectedHeavyBorder` compares the selected card with a hand grid: a heavy accent border `┏━┓┃┗┛`, with the inner cells of Components.dc.html line 71. `TestCardGolden` keeps the two unselected cards against the Components sheet. The sheet still shows the rounded selected card.

`internal/tui/component/fit_test.go` (repair): `checkBorderOf` checks a border of a given `lipgloss.Border`. `TestFitWave4` checks the unselected card with the rounded border and the selected card with the heavy border.

`internal/tui/frame_test.go`: `TestHelpMatchesItsBoard` now compares the whole Help grid cell for cell. Before, it compared only the box and the tokens (AC1).

`internal/tui/agents_test.go`: `TestAgentsPeekReadsInCommandAndEscClosesIt` now peeks the selected manager `w1:p3`, because the graph has no coordinator card.
