## Interaction runs

Each run used the fixture `new-grids/fixtures/ChatAskFlow.fixture.json`
(120x40) or `CompactChatAskFlow.fixture.json` (80x24). The fixture has 2
asks. Steps and captures are in `v3/flow-120x40/` and `v3/flow-80x24/`.

1. `1 4`, text, `enter`: question 2 (`2-question2`).
2. `tab`, text `small`, `enter`: the review opens with `› Send` and the rows
   `Scope → Mouse wheel (REC), Header count` and
   `Order → Modes first (REC) · note: small` (`3-review`).
3. `↑`, `↑`: the selection moves to Order, then Scope; the border shows
   `enter change` (`4-highlight-order`, `5-highlight-scope`).
4. `enter`: the Scope options with their picks (`6-edit-scope`).
5. `2 enter`: the review again, `› Send`, and
   `Scope → Mouse wheel (REC), Click to focus, Header count`. The `note` of
   Order stays (`7-return-review`).
6. Digits `1` to `5`, `space`, `tab`, text `az`, `backspace`, `i`,
   `ctrl+o`, `?`, `esc`, `←`, `→`, `pgup`, `pgdn`: `8-after-ignored-keys`
   equals `7-return-review` byte for byte, at both widths.
7. `J`: the second card (`9-J`). `K`: the first card in its review with
   its draft; `10-K` equals `7-return-review` byte for byte, at both widths.
8. `enter` on Send: the demo notice `demo mode: no action` (`11-enter-send`).
   V4 records the real send.
9. New run, `1 enter 2`: the review (`12-review-again`). `x`: the demo
   notice (`13-x-dismiss`). V4 records the real dismiss.

Control: `v3/control-120x40/`. Outside the review, `?` opens Help and `i`
opens the input. So the keys of step 6 reach the TUI, and the review
ignores them.

Clicks: `v3/click-120x40/` (`ChatAskReviewMulti`) and `v3/click-80x24/`
(`CompactChatAskReview`). A left click at columns 5 and 20 of each body row
of the review card (SGR mouse sequences): `2-after-clicks` equals `1-start`
(`result.txt`). Control: a click on the feed gives `[normal]`
(`3-control-feed-click`).

Long rows: `v3/tall-120x40/` (`ChatAskReviewTall`, a `note` of 900 words)
and `v3/tall-80x24/` (`CompactChatAskReviewTall`, 300 words). `↓` pages
through the row, then selects Send. `↑` goes back to the first line.
`summary.txt` at each width: no word is missing, and `MIDDLE-SENTINEL` and
`FINAL-SENTINEL` show.

Wrapping and full text: `captures/ChatAskReviewLong-120x40` and
`captures/CompactChatAskReviewLong-80x24` (5 picks, long Other text, 2 long
`note` values). Nothing is cut.

Result: pass. Each approved grid matches, and each interaction follows AC1
to AC3.
